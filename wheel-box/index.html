Wheel Pattern

Base Turn G is the G asked for (> 1, up to 3 G). It is flown when the 90° Base Turn fits inside Base Distance; otherwise the smallest G up to 3 G that fits is flown and the Result shows both. If no G up to 3 G fits, there is no Min Wheel Radius and the route cannot connect.

Wheel Radius MIN follows Min Wheel Radius, the smallest radius that flies the route: the Base Turn's transfer + Roll-in Long. D (a zero Base Leg), or the Wheel turn's load factor limit radius when that is larger. Base Distance is not a floor. A typed radius is kept; below the minimum it shows the required radius and an error. Clear the field to go back to MIN.

Wheel Turn ADV

The Wheel turn readout (bank, 180° time) and its source-bounded limits (5.5 G, Mach 0.95; M82 / M84 only, other weapons not evaluated) use this speed and altitude; they do not move the attack route.

Orbit ADV

Orbit is the waiting / deconfliction / targeting circle around the Target. It is not part of the attack route; the Top View shows it in Advanced.

Return ADV

After a CSEM (SEM End CLIMB), the Return flies from the CSEM End back into the Wheel at Abeam (BOX OUT physics, MIL, PW-229 research model, unofficial): it climbs on at the CSEM angle until 350 KCAS (the speed falls), turns toward the Wheel side at 90° bank and Return Turn G while the path falls to level (or the speed to 300 KCAS), then goes on at the G that ends the turn on the Abeam line (Downwind heading), holding 300 KCAS by descending where MIL cannot hold it, and flies the Downwind to Abeam getting back the Roll-in Altitude only (a MIL climb at the speed it has, or a descent when above); the speed at Abeam is not set. When the altitude would not be back by Abeam, it first gains altitude straight ahead (the Extension), so the path ahead lengthens. It never goes wider than the Wheel Radius. The Abeam Extension is flown to OA2, the Base Turn start (a visual reference: the time to it depends on the speed at Abeam).
