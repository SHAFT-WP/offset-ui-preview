BOX Pattern

Crosswind Leg Extension is filled in automatically: the leg flown at MIL from the OUT Turn end until the Roll-in Altitude and Roll-in speed are reached (never shorter than the leg that closes the pattern). A typed value replaces it; shorter than the automatic leg it shows a warning, below the minimum an error. Clear the field to go back to automatic.

OUT at MIL: climb at the Climb Pitch until the OUT Turn Speed, then the OUT Turn at the Turn Bank and Turn G; when the flight path reaches level the turn goes on level at the Turn G. Speeds come from the PW-229 MIL research model (unofficial adoption, not BMS-validated).
