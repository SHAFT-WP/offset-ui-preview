Wheel Pattern

The Wheel is the Target-centred circle flown at the Initial speed and the Roll-in Altitude (Official Wheel Rev0.4). The Roll-in starts on it, tangent: the Angle-Off is solved so that the Target is abeam of the Roll-in Start, and the Wheel Radius is then the Roll-in Range. Neither is typed: the BDP Profile and the Roll-in Condition decide them. Aircraft Count spaces the aircraft evenly on the Wheel, one Wheel time / N apart.
